Batman

Character Information

Real Name: Bruce Wayne

Universe: DC

Role: Superhero

Species: Human

Home: Gotham City

Backstory

Bruce Wayne became Batman after losing his parents when he was young. He trained himself physically and mentally so he could protect Gotham City and fight crime.

Abilities

  • Martial arts
  • Detective skills
  • Advanced technology
  • Strategy
  • Physical training

First Appearance

Detective Comics #27

Personality

Batman is serious, intelligent, and determined. He is very disciplined and focused on protecting Gotham City.
